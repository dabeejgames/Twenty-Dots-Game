    THE LITTLE RULEBOOK

    Easy to start.
    Fun to figure out.

    01

    Pick your spot.

    Each card has a color and a coordinate. A3 puts a dot on row A, column 3. Play up to two cards, together or one at a time.

    02

    Make a connection.

    Line up 3 or more dots of one color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Collect the line to score. Replacing a dot also earns a point.

    03

    A little wild goes a long way.

    Yellow matches every color. Roll to place it at the start, and again when it’s collected or replaced. If your roll makes a line, roll again.

    04

    Make it count.

    Classic: first to 20 dots wins. Color collector: collect 5 of each regular color. Wild ambition: collect those plus 5 yellow dots. Finish early with End turn, or pass without playing.
    A LITTLE TACTICAL TWIST

    Make a power move.

    Power Play adds 20 cards to the regular deck: one Paint, Shift, Swap, Block, and Tax in each color.

    • ✦ Paint: Place the card’s color on any empty square.
    • ↗ Shift: Move a dot matching the card’s color one square up, down, left, or right into an empty space. No diagonal moves. Yellow cannot be moved.
    • ⇄ Swap: Exchange any two regular dots anywhere on the board. Both squares must contain dots; neither can be yellow. The card’s color does not matter, and the two dots may be the same or different colors.
    • ⛔ Block: Block an empty square until your next turn begins. Nobody can play a card or power there, and the wild dice skip it. The card’s color does not matter.
    • ¤ Tax: Take up to two dots of the card’s color from an opponent tied for the most dots. Nobody gains them, and you can’t tax anyone while you lead alone.

    Roll first if required. Play one power instead of your two regular cards, before playing anything else. Target, preview, then confirm. Paint, Shift, and Swap score completed lines normally, not for moving or placing alone. Swap checks both spots and collects each dot only once. Collecting yellow still requires a roll unless you win.
