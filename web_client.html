    THE LITTLE RULEBOOK

    Easy to start.
    Fun to figure out.

    01

    Pick your spot.

    Each card has a color and a coordinate. A3 puts a dot on row A, column 3. Play up to two cards, together or one at a time.

    02

    Make a connection.

    Line up 3 or more dots of one color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Collect the line to score. Replacing a dot also earns a point.

    03

    A little wild goes a long way.

    Yellow matches every color. Roll to place it at the start, and again when it’s collected or replaced. If your roll makes a line, roll again.

    04

    Make it count.

    Classic: first to 20 dots wins. Color collector: collect 5 of each regular color. Wild ambition: collect those plus 5 yellow dots. Finish early with End turn, or pass without playing.
